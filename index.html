Questions

Straight answers

No. Text-back works with the mobile number your customers already have. Nothing changes for them.

Am I tied into a contract?+

The first 3 months are fixed so the setup has time to show results. After that it's month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

Does it work for dental and medical practices?+

Yes. I link your website and text-backs to the booking system you already use. The AI receptionist only takes messages and books appointments. It never gives clinical advice, and it collects only the details you need, in line with your data protection policy.

Do you only work locally?+

No. Everything happens online. We talk on Google Meet or by phone, and I set everything up remotely, wherever you are. I build websites in English or French.

Who owns the website?+

You do. The domain is registered in your name. If you ever leave, I hand everything over.

I already have a website. Can I keep it?+

Yes. The Callback Kit adds the automations to the site you have and tidies up anything that's costing you enquiries.

How much of my time does it take?+

About an hour in total: the first call, sending a few photos of your work, and one check before it goes live.
