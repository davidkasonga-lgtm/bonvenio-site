Questions trades ask

I'm up a ladder all day. How does text-back help?+

When you can't answer, the caller gets a text within seconds saying you're on a job and asking what they need. They reply by text, you read it when you're free, and they don't ring the next trader on the list.

I already get reviews on Checkatrade or TrustATrader. Why Google?+

Most people search on Google first, and Google reviews show on the map and search results. Automatic review requests ask every happy customer for a Google review with a direct link.

Do I need a website if I get work from directories?+

A simple site of your own, with your best jobs, your area and a quote form, means people who find you on Google can contact you directly, without a directory taking a cut or showing them your competitors.
