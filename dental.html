For practices

Questions practices ask

Can it link to our practice booking system?+

Yes. Your website's booking button and the text-backs point to the booking system you already use, so nothing changes for your team.

Will the AI receptionist give advice to patients?+

No. It only takes messages and books or requests appointments. It never gives clinical advice, and urgent calls are flagged to you straight away.

What about patient data?+

Forms and the receptionist collect only the details you need, in line with your data protection policy, and I add a plain-English privacy notice.

How much does it cost for a practice?+

Most practices choose the Business Site at £1,295 setup and £149 a month, often with the AI Receptionist at £149 a month plus £195 setup for evenings and busy times. Founding rates apply to the first 3 businesses.

Is it only for dentists?+

No. It suits dental practices, private GPs, aesthetics clinics, physios, osteopaths, podiatrists and therapists: any practice where calls come in while you're with a patient.
