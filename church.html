Questions

Straight answers

Can our volunteers update it themselves?+

Yes. On the Church Hub I train two people to change service times, notices and events. Or just send me the changes and I'll do them.

Can we take online giving?+

Yes. I connect the giving provider you already use, or help you choose one. Donations go straight to the church's account. I never handle the money.

Can you show our livestream and sermons?+

Yes. If you stream to YouTube or publish a podcast, the latest talks appear on the site without anyone copying them over.

Can you build our website in French?+

Yes. I build church websites in English, in French, or in both, with a language switch for bilingual congregations.

What about data protection?+

Forms only ask for what you need, and I add a privacy notice in plain English. Your safeguarding policy can sit on the site too, so people can find it.

We already have a website. Can you fix it instead?+

Often, yes. I'll look at it on our first chat and tell you honestly whether it's worth keeping.

Who owns the website?+

The church does. The domain is registered in the church's name, and if you ever move on I hand everything over.
